## Decisions
- 2026-10-28 — P8.4 integration: Reduce Motion for Customize. MotionConfig `reducedMotion="user"` only jumps transforms, so the editor's frame box and picker clip still animated (picker wiped in from the top). Now under Reduce Motion the editor fades in / out in place (0.25s, same length as the morph so the bar hand-off lines up), track cards fade without growing / sliding (a removed card closes up after its fade), the drop target cuts between bar and slot, and every imperative spring (strip paging, ghost, pull-up snap) goes through `reducible()` in tokens/motion.ts → a cut. A dropped picker icon fades where it was let go; a cancelled drag cuts home. The finger-driven parts still follow the finger. Rail chicklets for apps without a widget were already built in 8.0 (app icon on the chicklet card, Collapsed 92² and Driving 200²) — no Figma design for those, now covered by `widgets-placeholder-driving-*` shots. Driving: was Parked-only — superseded the same day, Customize works while Driving too (see Feedback). Persistence was 8.0 (localStorage, URL wins).
- 2026-10-28 — Easier drop targeting (`drag.ts`): gaps targeted from the finger (was the shrunken card's centre). Reach: picker apps 215px from a gap (was 150; a ~66px dead zone stays over the centred card, Q20), held cards 330 (any gap in view). Drop band: 110 above the edit frame to 50 below it, 60 past its sides (was 40 above / dots; no side limit). Only gaps inside the frame count. Edge zone 90px in from each end (was 24) and it no longer hides the gap under the finger; it pages only if there's a page that way, after 0.35s while no gap is lit (a held card's two visible gaps are its own, so every reorder starts like this) or 1s while one is (no overshoot). Slot opens after 0.3s (was 0.35).
- 2026-10-28 — Picker "on the track" marker (option D, not in Figma yet): 24×4 pill, `--color-status-info` (same blue as the insertion bar; Dark #8fb4ff), centred 3px above the tile's bottom edge — inside its 12px padding, so the picker keeps its Figma height. Grows in / out with scaleX + opacity (`transition.toggle`) as apps are added or removed.
- 2026-10-28 — Moves look like inserts: a moved app gets a new card key (`drag.ts` `gen` / `trackKey`), so it expands into the gap like a new one; its old card exits with `transition.cut` (AnimatePresence `custom` = old key). If the old card was left of the centred one, the strip jumps one card right in the same frame (layout effect) so nothing visible shifts, then pages to the new card — paged explicitly, because the page number can stay the same (left-of-centre → right-of-centre). Applies to picker drops and hold-to-reorder alike.
- 2026-10-28 — P8.3 drag & drop (`Customize/drag.ts`, transient state in its own small zustand store — not deep-linked). Picker: an app's 48px icon lifts after 8px of travel (tap does nothing); its tile goes empty but keeps its place (the sketch re-centres the row; keeping it avoids a jump and gives the icon a home to float back to). Target = nearest gap within 150px of the finger in a band from 40px above the edit frame to its dots; cards are never targets (Q20). Nearing a gap → 13×144 status/info bar, neighbours move apart to a 50px gap; 0.35s on the same gap → 79×158 slot at 40% (110px gap) and a picker icon settles into it (springSnap). Drop on a gap → insert/move + page to it; a picker icon fades into the new card, a dragged card grows back into place. Duplicate next to its own position → page to it, card blinks 3× (`presets.flash`, 3 × duration.slow), icon floats back. Past the outer gaps toward the frame edge for 0.55s → the track pages (repeats). Reorder: hold a card 0.3s still (Q19) → lifts to 1.04 with the panel shadow; once it moves it shrinks to 0.6 around the finger so the gaps stay visible; its own place stays empty until the drop; the gaps either side of it aren't targets. New tokens: `--color-status-info` (#005aff / Dark #8fb4ff from Figma), `presets.flash` + `transition.flash`.
- 2026-10-28 — Customize in Full stays Full (Anastasiya; reverses the 8.2 assumption that Full drops to Panel). ## Learnings
- 2026-10-28 — P8.4 review sweep (fidelity + function): no blockers. Fixed: a reduced-motion constant declared after use. Checked and not a bug: "?widgets= overwrites the saved track" (only an edit saves — verified with four deep links; the reviewer had edited the track). Left as is: revealed (not editing) doesn't close Quick Actions / Settings (it's just a button); in Full the lifted bar sits 64px above the trip card while revealed; Light Driving placeholder chicklet is low-contrast white-on-pale (no design); the frosted frame's blur shows the white car as a soft smudge in Dark (from the Figma blur).
- 2026-10-28 — Sampling `getComputedStyle` in rAF catches a one-frame "start value" at the end of every motion fade (WAAPI finished, final style not yet written) — it's not painted (no blink on iPad, and it shows on long-shipped fades too). Check a suspected blink with a screencast before "fixing" it.
- 2026-10-28 — "Front" moves failed because the edge-paging zone sat on top of the first gap and kept winning even with nothing left to page to; and gaps off past the frame were targetable once the edge zone stopped masking them. Zones that overlap targets need a priority rule, and hit tests should only consider what's on screen.
- 2026-10-28 — P8.3 review: a gesture check that only reads the URL can pass while the screen is wrong (edge paging updated `widget=` but the strip didn't move under a lifted card). Assert what's on screen too (card position), not just state. Also: capturing the pointer on a wrapper starves the child button of pointerup — clear its press state yourself.
- Capture motion with CDP `Page.startScreencast` (real time, frame timestamps); Playwright's fake clock froze motion's animations mid-way. No ffmpeg on this Mac.
- Pointer events fire before touch events, so a touchmove-based decision lands one move after the pointermove that crossed the slop.
- iOS Safari decides native scrolling on the first touchmove; a `touch-action: pan-x` list will cancel a slightly diagonal pointer drag (`pointercancel`). Claim the gesture with a non-passive `touchmove` + `preventDefault`. Chrome/CDP touch doesn't reproduce this — verify on the iPad.
- Before driving a motion value from the finger, `stop()` it: a running spring keeps writing and fights `set()`.
- Window capture-phase `pointerdown` + `stopPropagation` runs before React's root listener, so a "dismiss-only" tap never reaches the app; also swallow the click that follows (once, short window).
- In a drag that locks after a slop, don't `return` on the locking move — coalesced fast flicks may never send another move.
- CSS `mask-image` clips to the element's own border box, so masking a blurred element cuts its blur off in a hard edge. Put the mask on a larger wrapper (like Figma's mask group) and the blurred shape inside it.
- Swipe-to-close on a Motion element: drive `y` with a `useMotionValue` (start it at the enter offset so the open animation still runs), capture the pointer only after a small threshold so child taps keep working, and spring back with `animate(y, 0, …)`.
- Don't lay out big images in a CSS grid with `1fr` tracks: `1fr` has an `auto` minimum, and Safari sized the tracks from the images' natural 2940px, so the live map looked warped on iPad (Chrome was fine). Give each map image explicit px boxes.
- Render-map framing: the route must end inside the 1960 tile or the pin is unreachable (pan is clamped to the tile). If you move `DESTINATION`, adjust `CAR_FRACTION` so the car stays at `CAR_TILE_PX` on the same road.
- Figma Screens "Read me" documents the whole prototype wiring and motion tokens — treat as the UX spec.
- Display = 1920×1080 stage (Left 630 / Main / Overlay slots) + 120px control bar = 1920×1200.
- Tokens are clean Figma variables (`--color-*`, `--space-*`, `--radius-*`, `--font-size-*`, `--size-*`) with Light/Dark modes; Inter font.
- Screens page metadata is ~170k chars — never fetch whole pages; target node IDs. Foundations page (`342:18279`) is small and has the full Motion spec.
- Motion spec (Foundations): instant 100 / fast 150 / base 250 / slow 400 / deliberate 600ms; standard `cubic-bezier(.2,0,0,1)`, enter `(0,0,.2,1)`, exit `(.4,0,1,1)`, spring m1 k300 c30.
- Figma letterSpacing values are percent (1 → 0.01em). Map labels use Source Sans 3; everything else Inter.
- Primitive yellow/500 `#FFBD0A` (Figma's route line; we use primary blue now) and teal/500 have no semantic token yet.
- Stage: centering a 1920px box with CSS grid fails when it's wider than the viewport (track grows) — use absolute + translate(-50%,-50%) scale().
- Repo renamed to `Tesla-Experiments` (local folder + remote URL keep old spelling; GitHub redirects). Pages must be enabled (Settings › Pages › Source: GitHub Actions) or deploy-pages fails.
- Git CLI on this Mac has no GitHub login (no gh, no SSH key); Anastasiya pushes via GitHub Desktop. Phase 0 pushed 2026-10-26 (`84cc715`).
- Status bar items are split in Figma: PRND + battery live in the Car panel; lock/time/temp/profile/airbag in the Map Top Bar.
- Map holds turn list + trip card at 0% opacity (offset 40px) so State=Driving morphs them in — mirror this in P3.
- Control Bar is pinned to Dark in Figma → `data-mode="dark"` on its root.
- Glyph SVGs come with mode-resolved colors → render via `Icon` (CSS mask) + token color. Map/top-bar icons = `--color-icon-strong`.
- Component SVG exports carry page background + doc card → see COMPONENTS.md "Asset notes".
- Motion layout/layoutId animations measure in screen px → wrong inside the scaled Stage. Use explicit `animate` targets in design px; measure with offsetWidth (unscaled).
- Figma inconsistency: Car Status Panel description says Collapsed = 220 (size/panel/collapsed) or 102, but the Expanded screen uses 116. Built 116.
- Car Status Panel keeps Panel + Full layers in one component (hidden layers at 0%) — the metadata of the Car status instance gives Full rects directly.
- Widget bar: Music collapses to a 382×64 pill (Spotify · title · ↑); music Mini/Chicklet sizes exist for Collapsed panel (P4).
- `docs/ICONS.md` = full icon node index (Icons page is big; don't re-read).
- Vite inlines small SVGs as data URLs — select images by class, not by `src`, in tests.
- A broad `.tiles img { width: 100% }` rule stretched the route image (wrong angle) — scope image rules to their class.
- Nav maneuver icons are two-tone → `ThemedSvg` (raw SVG with hex → token swap); masks only work for one color.
- Pointer capture on the panel steals `click` from child buttons — controls must opt out of the panel gesture *before* capture.
- Playwright role queries ignore `inert`; scope locators (e.g. to the Map region) in tests.
- Scrolling flex columns squash children (flex-shrink 1) instead of scrolling — set `flex-shrink: 0` on scroll-body children.
- In a page, an element with `id="map"` is also `window.map` (named access) — don't use that name for globals.
- `npm run shots` uses installed Google Chrome via playwright-core (no browser download).
